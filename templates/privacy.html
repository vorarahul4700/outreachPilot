1. Introduction

{{ project_name }} ("we", "our", or "us") provides autonomous lead prospecting, business intelligence enrichment, and personalized cold outreach tools. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you access or use our application.

2. Information We Collect

  • Google Account Information: When you connect your Google Account via OAuth 2.0, we collect your email address and profile name to authenticate your account.
  • Resume & Profile Data: Candidate skills, experience, and target roles parsed from uploaded resume documents to generate personalized outreach pitches.
  • Outreach Logs: Timestamps, recipient addresses, subject lines, and message bodies of emails dispatched through your connected account.

3. Google API Data & Gmail Scope Disclosure

Our application requests access to the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send scope. This restricted permission is utilized solely to send personalized job applications and business outreach emails strictly and explicitly initiated by you.

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance:
{{ project_name }}'s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We never sell your Google user data to third parties.
  • We never use Google user data for advertising, credit scoring, or data aggregation.
  • We do not read, inspect, or store incoming inbox messages; our scope is strictly limited to sending emails you review and approve.

4. Data Security & Encryption

OAuth tokens and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest using industry-standard Fernet (AES-128-CBC) symmetric encryption before being stored in our PostgreSQL database. Uploaded resume files are immediately deleted from disk after text extraction.

5. Managing and Revoking Permissions

You may revoke {{ project_name }}'s access to your Google Account at any time via your Google Security Settings. Upon revocation, our servers will no longer have access to send emails on your behalf.
