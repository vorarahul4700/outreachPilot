01

Overview & Privacy Architecture

Core Commitment
infoKey Takeaway: OutreachPilot processes candidate information and company data strictly to synthesize and send your approved outreach.

{{ project_name }} ("we", "our", or "us") is a job search tool for individual job seekers. It helps you find companies you want to work for, reads your resume to understand your skills, and drafts a job application for each company that you review and send from your own Gmail. It is not a sales, marketing or lead generation tool, and commercial outreach is not allowed (see our Terms of Service). This Privacy Policy details the exact types of data we collect, how information is encrypted, and your sovereign rights over personal information.

04

Cryptographic Security & Storage

Infrastructure Security
lockKey Takeaway: OAuth tokens are symmetrically encrypted at rest with Fernet (AES-128-CBC) before database writes.

All OAuth refresh tokens and credentials are encrypted at rest using industry-standard Fernet (AES-128-CBC) symmetric cryptography before persisting to the database. Uploaded resume files are parsed in memory and wiped from disk immediately upon skill extraction.

05

Revoking Access & Sovereign Data Deletion

User Controls

You retain sovereign control over your permissions. You may revoke {{ project_name }}'s authorization at any moment through your Google Security Dashboard. Upon revocation, our servers are immediately barred from dispatching emails or accessing your profile. You may also request complete erasure of your audit logs anytime.
