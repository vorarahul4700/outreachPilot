01

How paid plans work

{{ project_name }} sells digital plans that raise how many job applications you can send each day. Each plan is a single payment made through Razorpay:

  • Weekly passes last 7 days from the moment the payment is confirmed.
  • Monthly plans last 30 days from the moment the payment is confirmed.
  • If you buy the same plan again while it is still active, the new days are added after your current end date.

When a plan ends, your account moves back to the Free plan automatically. Nothing is delivered by post or courier; the plan is available in your account right after payment.

02

Cancellation

Plans do not renew and we never save your card or UPI details for future charges, so there is no subscription to cancel. If you do not want to continue, simply do not buy again when your plan ends.

03

Refunds

Once a plan is active in your account, the payment is not refundable, including for days you do not use.

You get a full refund in these cases:

  • You were charged but the plan did not turn on in your account.
  • You were charged more than once for the same purchase.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay. The money usually reaches you within 5 to 7 working days after we start the refund; your bank may take longer to show it.

04

How to ask for a refund

Email founder@outreachpilot.in within 7 days of the payment. Include the email address you sign in with and the Razorpay payment ID from your payment receipt. We check the payment and reply by email.

See the Contact page for other ways to reach us, and our Terms of Service for the full terms of use.
