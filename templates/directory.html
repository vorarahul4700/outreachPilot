About the OutreachPilot B2B Directory

How does OutreachPilot verify technology stacks and company data? expand_more

OutreachPilot utilizes an autonomous multi-pass crawler that directly analyzes live company website source code, inspecting meta tags, API endpoints, script bundles, and DNS records to detect actual production frameworks (such as Python, React, Flutter, and Django) rather than relying on self-reported claims.

Can I contact the recruiters and engineering leads of these companies? expand_more

Yes. OutreachPilot extracts verified contact emails from career portals, header links, and domain DNS records, and enables users to draft hyper-personalized opportunity pitches sent directly through your authorized personal Gmail via OAuth 2.0.

How often is the directory intelligence updated? expand_more

Company profiles are re-crawled continuously to detect technology stack changes, new job openings, domain health, and Google review updates.
